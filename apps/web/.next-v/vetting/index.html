What a badge still cannot do

The checks above are reads. They are not proofs, and the difference is the whole of the remaining work.

Due diligence

Vetting proof-of-concepts

Not built
Would have
The second half of "they flag, we prove": each badge finding shipping a Foundry script that demonstrates it on a mainnet fork, so a claim about a pool is executable rather than assertable.
Why it is not here
The badge itself is built — `python -m misquote.vetting` reads nine checks off chain and writes vetting/badges/<pool>.json — and the fork lab under vetting/forge exists and runs. Nothing joins them: a FAIL today is a sentence, not a transaction that reverts.
